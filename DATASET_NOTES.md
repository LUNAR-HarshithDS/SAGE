# Dataset notes

The dataset teaches behavior rather than Qwen's existing knowledge.

Main behaviors covered:

- concise conversation
- cockpit terminology
- dry humor
- correction and truthfulness
- uncertainty
- hardware honesty
- safety
- persistent-memory boundaries
- technical explanations
- aviation terminology
- instruction following
- response-length control
- avoiding customer-service-style endings

The training examples deliberately avoid exposing chain-of-thought. The desired pattern is that simple requests receive simple answers rather than a narrated internal analysis.
